PRODUCT 001 · TECHNICAL DETAILS

WireDock V1

A small organizer for a less cluttered RC workbench.

Schematic illustration of the layout, not a photograph of a finished print. The STL model is the source file for printing.

What it does

WireDock is a one-piece bench accessory: a flat base with five long parallel rails that form four open-ended lanes. It is intended to help keep loose small wires organized during stationary work.

Important: This prototype is not an aircraft component. It is not designed, tested, or rated for flight, structural loads, or retaining wires inside a moving aircraft.
