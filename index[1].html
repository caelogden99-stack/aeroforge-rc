WHAT IT IS

A simple organizer for the RC bench.

WireDock is designed to sit on a workbench and separate loose small wires into four open-ended lanes. The prototype is a single flat base with five long rails running along its length.

It is intended for bench organization only. It has not been tested or designed to retain wires in a moving aircraft.
